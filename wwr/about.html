WHO WE ARE

Built on a Passion for the River

Since 1998, Whitewater Rafting Adventures has helped people discover the excitement, beauty, and challenge of whitewater rafting.

Whether it is your first time on the water or your next big expedition, our goal is to make every journey safe, welcoming, and unforgettable.

OUR STORY

From Two Rafts to Countless Memories

Founded in 1998, Whitewater Rafting Adventures began with two rafts, a love of river exploration, and a simple ambition: to share the thrill of the water with others.

Over the years, our adventures have brought together families, friends, and explorers looking for something beyond the ordinary.

Our spirit remains the same today: respect the river, look after one another, and make every trip count.
